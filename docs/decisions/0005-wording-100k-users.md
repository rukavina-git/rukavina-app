# 0005: Wording of the 100k+ users claim

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-08

## Question

How is the 100k+ users claim presented when it can't be linked or proven because of NDA?

## Options

1. State it plainly as a headline stat
2. State it with NDA context, inside the work section
3. Fold it into a specific case study as one detail
4. Omit it

## Constraints

- Confirmed true; cannot be proven (NDA).
- CLAUDE.md bans the centered hero plus stats row pattern.
- Unknown: whether 100k+ is one app or several combined.

## Council

| Seat | Pick | Reasons | Main risk | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client (audience: engineering managers) | 2 | A bare number looks like template filler; NDA context explains the missing proof and signals discretion | Skeptical readers still discount it | Medium |
| Designer | 2, one line near the top in body type | A stats row is banned; open NDA context reads credible | Buried too deep, the 5-second test misses it | Medium |
| Sales and marketing | 2, paired with NDA case studies | A bare stat is ignored; NDA context sells trust for security work | Wording mustn't imply one app if it was several | Medium |
| Skeptic | 3 | Repeating "NDA" signals "can't show anything"; any standalone number recreates the stat pattern | Scale wording per project may be identifying | Medium |

## Recommendation

Option 2, three of four seats, with the skeptic's guard rails:

- One sentence in body type on the first screen, never a large numeral. For example: "Apps I've built have reached over 100,000 users, mostly government platforms under NDA."
- The same scale repeated as a detail inside the relevant case study.
- "NDA" is said once per context, not on every card.
