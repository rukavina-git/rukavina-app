# 0008: Council review of homepage mock Q2 (scroll story)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-10

## Question

Is Q2 ready to become the chosen homepage direction, and if not, what must change first?

## Seats (set by Karlo for this run)

Critic, Sales, Engineering manager hiring a contractor, Client looking for a freelancer (startup founder). Each seat reviewed the exported mock (`design/review/q2*.png`) independently.

## Council

| Seat | Verdict | Works | Must change | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critic | Ready with changes | Hero passes the 5-second test; "What I do" rows; restrained palette | "How I work" is credentials, not process; text slide-in on every part is the banned fade-up; NDA stage panels repeat the text and project list looks disabled; loose spacing, form gives no prompt | Medium |
| Sales | Ready with changes | Clear offer; one CTA with risk reducers beside it; low-friction contact | Proof-sentence wording; rewrite "How I work" as how an engagement runs; second quiet CTA after Work; Contact in nav as an outline button; "Send message" plus a confirmation state | Medium |
| Engineering manager | Ready with changes | Clear hero; GymBuddy copy signals seniority; NDA panels credible | "How I work" should cover time zone, hours, engagement length, team fit, availability; lead Work with the strongest project; prove AI or move it down; add stack line and LinkedIn/GitHub links | Medium-high |
| Freelancer client | Ready with changes | Clear in 5 seconds; specific write-ups; calm; email and form | "How I work" should show the steps of working together; AI unproven; form needs a prompt and an after-send expectation; public projects first, NDA panels short | Medium |

## Recommendation

Q2 becomes the direction once these are done. All four seats agree on the verdict and on the first point.

1. Replace "How I work" with how an engagement runs: first call, scoping, communication and reporting, working-hours overlap (CET), availability, code ownership. Needs facts from Karlo.
2. Cut the slide-in on every part; keep the scroll line and the stage switch as the only motion.
3. Work: make the project list real, clickable anchors with an active state; decide the order (strongest proof first); keep NDA panels short; add public links (store page, live site).
4. Contact: a prompt in the form ("What are you working on?"), "Send message", a confirmation that repeats the 24-hour reply; a quiet second CTA after Work; Contact in the nav as an outline button.
5. AI in the headline: keep only if Karlo can name concrete AI work; otherwise lead with mobile and architecture until the AI project exists.
6. Optional: one-line stack summary, LinkedIn and GitHub links next to the email, a portrait.

Note: three seats flagged "mostly on government platforms" as overstated. That wording was approved by Karlo in 0005, so it stays unless he changes it.

## Decision

Karlo (2026-11-10): Accepted. The fixes were applied as Q3, which moves to the Decided page as the homepage direction. Facts given: engagement starts with a free first meeting (with the client or hiring manager); available now; LinkedIn and GitHub links next to the email; AI stays in the headline, with a fictional AI project in the mock until the real one exists (tracked in docs/launch-checklist.md); the government platform leads Work with a generic illustrative image. "Working together" copy approved.
