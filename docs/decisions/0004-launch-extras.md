# 0004: Extras at launch

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-08

## Question

Which extras ship at launch: blog, CV download, testimonials?

## Options

1. None
2. CV download only
3. CV plus blog
4. CV, blog and testimonials

## Constraints

- No testimonials exist. Content rules forbid placeholders or unconfirmed claims.
- No blog posts written.

## Council

| Seat | Pick | Reasons | Main risk | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client (audience: founders/CTOs) | 2 | A CV gets forwarded internally; an empty blog signals abandonment; no placeholder testimonials | Trust then rests entirely on case-study writing | High |
| Designer | 2, designed in the site's typography | Empty sections are the clearest unfinished-template signal; fewer sections give the work room | Site feels thin unless NDA case write-ups are deep | High |
| Sales and marketing | 2 | Without a CV the lead stalls in someone's inbox; a named engineering-manager testimonial is the strongest proof to collect | CV and site drift apart | High |
| Skeptic | 2, generated from the site's content | A stale blog is evidence of neglect; collecting testimonials must not delay launch | Nothing shows how he thinks | Medium |

## Recommendation

Option 2, unanimous. A one-page CV/profile PDF in both languages, built from the same content as the site so it can't drift. No blog until three posts are written. Start asking two or three past contacts (non-NDA) for testimonials now, and add them once they're confirmed. Depth goes into the case studies instead: problem, scale, constraints, and architecture diagrams Karlo draws himself.
