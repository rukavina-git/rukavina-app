# 0002: Services and pricing

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-08

## Question

Which services are listed, how are they named, and are prices shown?

## Options

1. Keep the four previous services (Mobile, Web for businesses, Architecture & Consulting, AI Integration) with prices
2. Three offers for the main audiences plus a separate plain-language business page, AI folded in as a capability, prices only on the business page
3. As 2, with rate ranges for contracting

## Constraints

- Strongest proof is Android/Kotlin and security-heavy NDA work.
- No confirmed AI project in the portfolio.
- No price figures confirmed by Karlo yet.

## Council

| Seat | Pick | Reasons | Main risk | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client (audience: agencies) | 2 | Day rates are negotiated; a standalone AI service from a mobile engineer looks trend-chasing; owners want a price signal | No visible rate makes some assume he's expensive | Medium |
| Designer | 3 | A four-card icon grid is a template tell; visible prices signal confidence | Ranges need confirmed numbers | Medium |
| Sales and marketing | 2 | Four equal services read as a generalist; specialist positioning sells to engineering managers | A "from" price set too low attracts bad leads | Medium |
| Skeptic | 2, team augmentation as its own offer | AI line is the most template-looking; restaurant sites next to Secure Element work dilute seniority; "from" prices anchor engineering managers low | Unqualified leads cost call time | Medium |

## Recommendation

Option 2, three of four seats.

1. **Mobile development**: contract or team augmentation, Kotlin first, iOS and cross-platform.
2. **Architecture and code review**: system reviews, security, CI/CD, audits, due diligence.
3. **Apps and websites for businesses**: its own page, no jargon, one "projects start from €X" line.

AI integration is listed as a capability under 1 and 2, not as a headline service. The contracting path says rates are quoted after a short call. Written as typeset sections with concrete deliverables, not an icon-card grid.

Strongest argument against: the designer's point that hiding rates everywhere looks generic. Showing the price on the business page addresses that where it matters most.

Answered by Karlo: no price yet (revisit later); no AI project yet, one is planned.

## Decision

Karlo: Accepted with changes. The first offer is named "Mobile development" (not "Android"). No prices at launch; whether and how to show a price is revisited later. Tone: a professional you would bring into a company and talk to, not a sales site. No AI project exists yet; one is planned, and AI stays a capability until then.

Amended by Karlo on 2026-11-08: AI integration is offered as a full service, not only a capability. The portfolio gets an AI entry only once the project exists.
