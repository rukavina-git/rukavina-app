# 0006: Which four new homepage directions to mock

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-08

## Question

Of seven candidate visual directions, which four should be mocked in Figma for Karlo to choose from, after he rejected Swiss grid and Security printing and called Standard "kinda okay"?

## Options

- G. Work up front: offer headline left, GymBuddy product shot right, work as rows with thumbnails (Hanken Grotesk, green).
- H. Letter: narrow column that reads like a short personal note, serif headline, email inline (Newsreader + DM Sans, rust).
- I. Colour hero: hero on one inset deep-blue panel with a gold button, work list below (Sora).
- J. Centred, expandable work: centred headline, pill button, work as an accordion (Outfit, indigo).
- K. Project showcase scroll: sticky intro left, one large tinted panel per project on the right.
- L. Mediterranean light: warm white, sea-blue accent, work on a sand band (Fraunces + Hanken Grotesk).
- M. How we'd work: offer headline, then a talk / plan / build strip before the work.

G, H, I and J were already built before the council ran.

## Constraints

- Karlo's feedback: no name as hero, the hero is about what he does; nothing that reads like a newspaper; readable; no gimmicks.
- Only GymBuddy and Adria VA can show visuals. NDA work never shows screenshots or identifying detail.
- No testimonials, logos or prices. No new claims without Karlo's confirmation.
- Banned patterns from CLAUDE.md; impeccable craft floor (no eyebrows, no unexplained numbering, no same-size card grids, no mono costume) and its reflex-font list.
- Assumption: the impeccable concept-seed roll did not run; candidates came from impeccable's new-work and craft-floor rules applied by hand, without PRODUCT.md on main (it is on the unmerged branch `claude/impeccable-product`).

## Recommendation

G, H, I and L. G and I were picked by all four seats, H and L by three. J was picked by none and is replaced by L. K and M each got one vote and stay out: K can't be filled with real material yet, and M needs copy Karlo hasn't approved.

Fixes the seats agree on:

- G: NDA projects as deliberate text rows (problem, scale, stack), not abstract thumbnails. Put "Free first consultation, reply within 24 hours" and the email next to the button; the 50,000+ figure sits on the government row. Check the GymBuddy shot doesn't make the hero read like a fitness app.
- H: replace Newsreader + DM Sans (reflex fonts), e.g. Source Serif 4 + Hanken Grotesk, and keep it clearly apart from the existing editorial serif variant.
- I: give it a face with more character than Sora; keep the panel off the banned dark-navy look (lighter or warmer blue) and the button calm, not salesy.
- L: replace Fraunces; push sea-blue and sand rather than cream; keep the offer as the hero so it doesn't read as a tourism page.

Open risk: all four are calm and light. If Karlo wants something further from Standard, the next round should come from a properly run impeccable direction roll.
