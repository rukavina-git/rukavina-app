# 0001: Personal brand

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-08

## Question

Should the site use a personal name brand or a studio-style brand?

## Options

1. Personal: "Ivan Karlo Rukavina"
2. Personal, short display name: "Karlo Rukavina", full name in About, metadata and CV
3. Studio-style: "Rukavina"

## Constraints

- Main audiences hire a person (CLAUDE.md audiences 1 to 3). Solo operator.
- Domain is `rukavina.app`, surname only.
- Assumption: Karlo goes by "Karlo" professionally. Not yet confirmed.

## Council

| Seat | Pick | Reasons | Main risk | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client (audience: engineering managers) | 2 | Hiring one person; matches LinkedIn/GitHub for vetting; studio from a solo operator reads inflated | Business owners may doubt one person can maintain their site | High |
| Designer | 2 | Faceless one-person studios are a template tell; name and type carry identity | Drifting into a résumé page | High |
| Sales and marketing | 2, with a one-line offer under the name | Buyers vet a person; studio name blocks job offers | Business owners may trust a "company" more | High |
| Skeptic | 2 | Full three-part name is long and hard to recall; top audiences still hire a person | Solo delivery risk for audience 4 | High |

## Recommendation

Option 2, unanimous. Wordmark "Karlo Rukavina", full name in About, metadata and the CV. A plain offer line sits under the name. The audience 4 page answers the "what if you're one person" question directly (maintenance, handover). The strongest argument against: the surname-only domain already reads like a studio, so the name on the page and the URL differ. All seats judged that acceptable.

## Decision

Karlo: Personal brand, accepted, using the full name "Ivan Karlo Rukavina" as the display name everywhere (not the short form).
