# 0007: Which four clean, contact-focused directions to mock

- Status: accepted (council overridden)
- Date: 2026-11-09

## Question

Of seven candidates, which four homepage directions should be mocked next under Karlo's new brief: clean, modern, organised, with everything leading the visitor to press "Get in touch"?

## Options

- G+. Work up front, refined (GymBuddy screens, NDA work as text rows, contact beside the button).
- I+. Colour hero, refined (calm blue panel that docks into a slim header keeping "Get in touch" visible).
- P. System map (line diagram of Mobile app, API, AI, Web; projects tagged by part).
- Q. Scroll stage (sticky stage that changes per project as you scroll).
- R. Contact always visible (sticky contact panel beside the content).
- S. Problem-led entry (three rows by visitor need, each with its own prefilled contact link).
- T. Featured case study (one project told by problem, scale, approach, result).

## Constraints

- Karlo (2026-11-09): "sales" means getting the visitor to press the contact button. No prices, no numbers pushed at the visitor, no payment. Tone stays calm. He agrees a dev portfolio should be clean and organised, not design-heavy. He earlier asked for the site to feel alive; treated as light, purposeful motion (assumption: he has not confirmed the level).
- Kept by Karlo: G and I. Rejected: Swiss grid, Security printing, letter, Mediterranean, bento, README, editorial serif, email as headline, dark big work rows.
- Only GymBuddy has screenshots, and none are on Karlo's Mac yet. NDA work never gets visuals. No AI project exists yet.
- New copy needs Karlo's approval.

## Council

| Seat | Picks (ranked) | Reasons | Main risk | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client (audience: engineering managers) | S, G+, T, I+ | S goes from "is this for me?" to an email fastest; G+ and T give specific proof; I+ keeps contact visible more quietly than R | AI in the headline with no AI project; S and T need new copy | Medium |
| Designer | G+, I+, T, S | Each has one clear idea; T and S organise rather than decorate; P looks like stock illustration, Q is the heavy design the brief rejects, R reads like a checkout | All four may look alike unless type, spacing and accent differ | Medium |
| Sales and marketing | S, G+, I+, T | S routes each buyer to contact; G+ and T carry the trust signals; I+'s docked header beats R's sticky form | Heavy reliance on GymBuddy as the only visual; S and T copy | Medium |
| Skeptic | S, G+, I+, T | S survives mobile and reduced motion with no invented visuals; P's AI node is an unbacked claim, Q's NDA diagrams are invented visuals and vanish on mobile | Stripped motion may feel too static next to "feel alive" | Medium |

## Recommendation

S, G+, I+ and T. All four seats chose the same four; three ranked S first. P, Q and R are dropped.

Fixes the seats asked for:

- S: evidence visible on every row by default, not only on hover; real proof in the rows (GymBuddy in the mobile row, the 50,000+ line in the team row). No AI row until an AI project exists. Prefilled email subjects in English and Croatian.
- G+: one GymBuddy screen shown straight on instead of the three-phone fan (common template move); no screen recording until real screenshots exist. Watch that GymBuddy doesn't brand him as a fitness-app developer.
- I+: keep the docking header with "Get in touch"; drop the ambient colour drift (reduced-motion and navy risk).
- T: the featured case uses NDA-safe wording; its "result" line stays empty until Karlo confirms one.
- All four: different typefaces and accents so they don't collapse into one generic look.

Open risk: with motion pared back, the set may feel static against "feel alive".

## Decision

Karlo (2026-11-09): Work on Q (scroll stage) as the base; the others move to an "Ideas" page in Figma. He likes Q's line that marks each section. Motion means something like P's map animated as a short loop of about five steps, nothing crazy. Show real work rather than an API diagram. No email next to the "Get in touch" button (redundant); the email belongs with his contact details.
